## 🔴 BREAKING: Apple sues OpenAI over trade secret theft

**Date:** July 10, 2026
**Source:** 9to5Mac, Hacker News (1125 points)
**Links:**
- [Apple sues OpenAI](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai-trade-secret-theft/)
- [OpenAI responds](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/10/openai-responds-to-apples-trade-secret-theft-lawsuit/)

**Summary:**
Apple filed a lawsuit against OpenAI in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, accusing former employees of stealing trade secrets "for the benefit of OpenAI."

**Key allegations:**
- Defendants include Chang Liu (ex-Apple engineer who joined OpenAI Jan 2026) and Tang Tan (ex-Apple VP of product design, joined Jony Ive's io)
- OpenAI acquired Jony Ive's io Products in a $6.5B deal, bringing 50+ ex-Apple engineers
- Employees allegedly emailed themselves confidential info when leaving Apple
- Liu kept Apple-issued laptop and exploited a vulnerability to download dozens of confidential documents
- Tan directed job candidates to bring Apple hardware prototypes/components to interviews
- OpenAI used confidential Apple info to approach Apple suppliers, tricking one into using a proprietary metal-finishing technique by claiming Apple permission
- Apple first raised concerns with OpenAI in February; OpenAI never responded

**OpenAI response:**
- Drew Pusateri (Director of Strategic Communications) stated: "We have no interest in other companies' trade secrets. We remain focused on building innovative technology that empowers people everywhere."

**Context:**
- This is not the first such dispute — hardware startup iyO also sued OpenAI/io over trade secrets (amended complaint March 2026)
- Major implications for Apple-OpenAI relationship and AI industry talent mobility
