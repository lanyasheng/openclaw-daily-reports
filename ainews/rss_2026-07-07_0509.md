## Spotlight: Anthropic "Global Workspace" (J-space)
Anthropic published a new paper identifying an emergent "J-space" in Claude — a small set of internal neural patterns that function similarly to the conscious/global workspace in neuroscience. Key findings:
- J-space patterns are reportable: Claude can describe what it's "thinking about"
- Modulable on request: you can ask Claude to think about something silently
- Used for internal reasoning: intermediate steps light up in J-space even when not spoken aloud
- NOT involved in most processing (fluent speaking, simple recall, grammar) — it's a dedicated reasoning subspace
- **Not designed by Anthropic** — emerged naturally during training
- Technique: Jacobian-based analysis

**Significance:** This is the first strong evidence that language models have spontaneously developed a neural architecture analogous to the conscious workspace theory (Baars, Dehaene). Important for AI interpretability and understanding model cognition.
