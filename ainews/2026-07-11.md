## ⚡ Major: Apple sues OpenAI for trade secret theft

**Story:** Apple filed a lawsuit against OpenAI, former Apple VP of Product Design Tang Tan, senior engineer Chang Liu, and io Products (Jony Ive's hardware startup acquired by OpenAI for $6.5B). Apple alleges a "pattern of theft" — ex-employees stealing confidential hardware info about unreleased technologies, manufacturing processes, and supplier relationships to advance OpenAI's consumer hardware efforts. The suit claims defendants encouraged candidates to bring Apple prototypes/components to interviews and approach Apple suppliers using proprietary info.

**OpenAI response:** Drew Pusateri (Dir. Strategic Comms) posted on X: "We have no interest in other companies' trade secrets. We remain focused on building innovative technology that empowers people everywhere."

**Context:** This is the second trade-secret dispute involving OpenAI's hardware efforts with Jony Ive. Tang Tan left Apple Feb 2024 to work with Ive; Chang Liu joined OpenAI Jan 2026 after 8 yrs at Apple.

**Signals:**
- Escalating Apple-OpenAI rivalry moving from partnership (Apple Intelligence) to litigation
- OpenAI's hardware ambitions (Ive-led) increasingly at center of legal risk
- Potential IPO timing for OpenAI now complicated by major lawsuit
