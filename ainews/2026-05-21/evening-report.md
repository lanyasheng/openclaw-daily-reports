### 3. 字节跳动发布 Lance：单模型统一图像/视频理解、生成与编辑
[来源](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/21/one-model-three-modalities-bytedance-releases-lance-for-image-and-video-understanding-generation-and-editing/) | MarkTechPost
