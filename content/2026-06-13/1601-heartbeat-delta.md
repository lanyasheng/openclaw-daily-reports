## 🚀 TOP STORY: Fable 5 / Mythos 5 — US Govt Export Control Directive

**Story ID:** #48511072 | **2,034pts**, ~1,500 comments | 7h old, still active

### What happened
Anthropic received a US Government directive at **5:21pm ET** to suspend **ALL** access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by any foreign national — including foreign national Anthropic employees. Since Anthropic can't practically enforce this distinction, they shut the models down globally.

**Government rationale:** Claims a jailbreak method was found that could bypass safeguards.

**Anthropic's response:**
- The alleged "jailbreak" is a technique to identify a small number of previously known, **minor vulnerabilities**
- These vulnerabilities are also present in **other publicly-available models** (no unique risk)
- Their safeguards are the **strongest of any deployed model**, tested by US/UK AISI + thousands of hours of red-teaming
- **No universal jailbreak** has been found
- Acknowledges perfect jailbreak resistance is impossible
