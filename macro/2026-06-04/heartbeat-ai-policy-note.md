# 2026-06-04 17:15 CST — Google AI Search attribution / publisher opt-out UK CMA signal

**Signal source:** AINews inter-session alert; source page fetched at 2026-06-04 17:14 CST.

## Facts
- Google will reportedly test Search Console controls allowing UK website owners to opt out of AI Overviews / AI Mode use while remaining eligible for traditional search.
- UK CMA-linked requirements include clearer attribution/source links and no penalty for publishers that opt out; reported compliance window is around 9 months.
- Google also plans Search Console reporting showing which pages appear in AI-generated responses.

## Macro/regulatory read-through
- **Regulatory relevance:** Medium. This is not a monetary/fiscal macro shock, but it strengthens the UK/EU-style platform bargaining-power / AI-content-rights regulatory track and could become a template for other jurisdictions.
- **Market transmission:** Low immediate beta impact; more relevant to Alphabet/search monetization risk premium, publisher revenue bargaining power, and compliance-product changes in AI search.
- **Tail risk:** If opt-out and attribution rules globalize, AI-search traffic economics may shift from pure model/product quality competition toward regulated content-access frameworks.

## Action boundary
