# 11:38 CST macro heartbeat alert — USTR Section 301 tariff proposal

**Time:** 2026-06-03 11:38 CST / 03:38 UTC  
**Trigger:** CNBC Economy RSS added a fresh 03:19 UTC headline: “U.S. proposes fresh tariffs on 60 economies over forced labor trade practices.” Primary source confirms USTR proposed Section 301 additional duties on imports from 60 economies.

## Facts

- USTR determined under Section 301 that 60 economies failed to impose/effectively enforce forced-labor import prohibitions and proposed additional duties on all products of the investigated economies except listed exclusions.
- Proposed tariff rates: **10%** for economies with full/partial forced-labor import prohibitions or related commitments; **12.5%** for all others.
- Affected list includes **China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, India, Vietnam, the EU, Canada, Mexico, the UK** and many others.
- This is a **proposal / public-comment process**, not immediate implementation: requests to appear due **2026-06-22**, written comments due **2026-07-06**, hearings on **2026-07-07**.
