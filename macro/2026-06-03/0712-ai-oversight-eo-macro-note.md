## Facts
- Trump signed a narrowed AI oversight EO.
- Strong AI model developers are asked to voluntarily submit models for government test/evaluation 30 days before release.
- Text reportedly does **not** establish mandatory licensing, pre-clearance, or permitting.
- DOJ prioritizes AI-assisted hacking / unauthorized access enforcement.
